Meet Cheri

Cheri Long

Associate Broker, Royal LePage® Commercial

Cheri has spent more than 30 years in Calgary commercial real estate, and most of her life in the city itself. That history shows up in the details: she knows which areas are growing, which buildings have stories, and who to call.

Most of her business comes from referrals. Clients come back, and send their colleagues, because she puts their goals first and tells them plainly what a property is worth and what a deal will take.
