Meet Cheri

Cheri Long

Associate Broker, Royal LePage® Commercial

Cheri has spent more than 30 years in Calgary commercial real estate, and most of her life in the city itself. She has watched neighbourhoods grow, industrial areas expand and business districts change, and that history shows up in the advice she gives: which areas are growing, which buildings have stories, and who to call.

Her business is built on referrals. Clients come back, and send their colleagues, because she puts their goals first and tells them plainly what a property is worth and what a deal will take.

Cheri works across every kind of commercial property: sales and purchases, landlord and tenant leasing, land and development sites, and the confidential sale of operating businesses. Whatever you're working on, you deal with her directly.
