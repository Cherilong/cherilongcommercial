01

Commercial & Industrial Sales / Buying

For: owners, owner-users and investors buying or selling office, industrial, retail and investment property.

Discuss a sale or purchase
  • Pricing you can trust. A clear opinion of value based on comparable sales, income and the current Calgary market.
  • Marketing that reaches buyers. MLS® and commercial listing exposure, plus direct outreach to Cheri's network of investors and agents.
  • Buyer search. We find properties that fit your use, budget and zoning, including ones that aren't widely advertised.
  • Due diligence support. Help gathering leases, financials, environmental and building reports, and coordinating inspections.
  • Negotiation to closing. Offers, conditions and timelines managed so the deal holds together.
02

Commercial Leasing

For: landlords filling space, and businesses looking for their first or next location.

Talk about leasing
  • Landlord representation. Marketing vacant space, screening tenants and negotiating terms that protect the building's value.
  • Tenant representation. Finding space that suits how your business works, from size and layout to parking, access and zoning.
  • Clear lease comparisons. Base rent, operating costs, improvement allowances and free rent laid side by side, so you see the true cost.
  • Renewals and expansions. Know your options before your lease comes up.
03

Land & Development Sites

For: landowners, builders and investors buying or selling raw land and development parcels in Calgary and the surrounding area.

Ask about land
  • Site search. Parcels matched to your intended use, size and servicing needs.
  • Land valuation. Pricing that accounts for zoning, location and what can realistically be built.
  • Seller marketing. Getting your land in front of the developers and investors most likely to act.
  • Working with your advisors. Coordination with planners, lawyers and lenders through conditions.
04

Business Brokerage & Private Advisory

For: owners selling an operating business, buyers looking for one, and property owners who want a confidential second opinion.

Start a confidential conversation
  • Confidential sales. Marketing your business without tipping off staff, customers or competitors.
  • Qualified buyers. Screening and confidentiality agreements before any details are shared.
  • Business and real estate together. Handling the sale of a business and the property it operates from, or the lease that goes with it.
  • Private advice. Straight answers on whether to hold, sell, lease or redevelop a property.
